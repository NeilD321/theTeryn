# Responsibility

*Trial chapter, version 6. Not approved text.*

---

**1** We have this world, this life and each other, and what we make of them is in our hands.

**2** Every life begins unwritten, and every life is written by the one who lives it.

**3** Some give their lives to fate, some to the stars, some to luck, and some to the voice that gives the orders; yet every life still belongs to the one who lives it.

**4** To say "this is just how I am" is itself a choice.

**5** Every kindness ever given was given by someone who chose to give it, and every cruelty was chosen too.

**6** The neighbour who brings bread to a house of mourning, the stranger who stops on the road, the friend who is first to say sorry: these are the works of people, freely done.

**7** Courage and cowardice, kindness and harm, belong to those who choose them.

**8** Where we are born, to whom, and in what body, all come before our first choice.

**9** Each of us begins where we find ourselves, and from there the path is ours.

**10** A single choice can turn a day, a friendship, and sometimes a whole life.

**11** Forgiveness for a wrong lives with the one who was wronged; it is theirs to give, and ours to ask.

**12** The reward for a good deed is found in the lives it touches, and it is paid in full the moment it is done.

**13** We are responsible for ourselves, and to each other.

**14** A weight carried together is lighter than a weight carried alone.

**15** We are each other's keepers.

**16** Whoever owns their choices owns their life.

**17** Every life is in human hands, and human hands are enough.

---

## Notes

**What changed in v6:**
- "There are no gods" was removed as the opening verse. The founder (11 Nov) found it too strong to open with: it would turn readers off immediately. Verses renumbered.
- Verse 17, "Every life is in human hands, and human hands are enough", added as the closing verse to carry the same meaning gently (chosen by the founder, 11 Nov).
- Verse 3 reworded ("still belongs to the one who lives it") to avoid repeating "hands".

**What changed in v5:**
- Every verse stands alone.
- The register is scriptural: declarative, timeless, built on parallel halves.
- Modern words; no contractions; no editorial chat.
- **Avoid "answer for"** (founder, 11 Nov), because it implies being called to account by someone above us.

**Checklist** (`EDITORIAL/checklist.md`):
- **Standalone test** passed.
- **No negatives** remain.
- **"We" throughout;** no commands.
- **No overclaims.**

**Biblical echoes:** verse 6, "house of mourning" (Ecclesiastes 7:2), and verse 15, "each other's keepers" (Genesis 4:9). The founder accepted both for now (11 Nov).

**Founder's line changed:** "That is not a burden. / It is the whole point." no longer appears.

**Open question:** does the founding line ("There are no gods. We have this universe…") also need softening for the opening of the whole book?
