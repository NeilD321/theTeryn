# Trial chapter: Responsibility

Status: **TRIAL DRAFT, NOT APPROVED TEXT.** Written 11 Nov 2026 to test the agreed style over a whole chapter:
- short verses, one per line, that read straight through;
- "state it, don't argue it";
- serious but warm;
- the central theme of responsibility.

No book is assigned yet. This is version 2, rewritten in the positive after the founder found version 1 "very negative".

---

1. There are no gods.
2. There is this world, this life, and each other.
3. Your life is yours to shape.
4. Every choice in it is yours.
5. It can be comforting to believe it was all decided long ago, and that our part is simply to follow.
6. People have handed their lives to many things: to gods and to fate, to luck, to the stars, to "meant to be", to whoever was giving the orders.
7. Some hand their lives to themselves, and say, "That's just how I am."
8. Yet every life is unwritten until it is lived.
9. You choose your lines, and you speak them yourself.
10. Every good thing you have ever seen was done by someone who chose to do it.
11. So was every cruelty.
12. The kindness and the harm, the courage and the excuses, belong to the people who chose them.
13. Some things simply happen.
14. Where you were born, to whom, the body you were given: these came first.
15. Everyone starts where they find themselves.
16. What is yours is what you do next.
17. It is enough to change a day, a friendship, a life.
18. When you do harm, forgiveness lives with the people you hurt.
19. It is theirs to give, and yours to ask for.
20. When you do good, the reward is the good itself, and the people it reaches.
21. This is a great deal to carry.
22. It is lighter when we carry it together.
23. We are responsible for ourselves, and to each other.
24. We have each other, so we look after each other.
25. Some call this a burden.
26. It is the whole point.

---

## Notes for discussion

- **Version 2 change:** version 1 had about 15 negatives ("no one", "did not", "no plan", "not a burden"). Version 2 keeps only verse 1, "There are no gods", and says what we *have* everywhere else.
- **Shape:**
  - 1–4: the plain statement, and what we have.
  - 5–7: the ways people hand their lives over.
  - 8–12: every life is unwritten; choices belong to those who make them.
  - 13–17: honesty about what simply happens.
  - 18–20: forgiveness and reward, both found among people.
  - 21–26: we carry it together, and that is the point.
- **No such thing as luck** (founder, 11 Nov 2026). Luck as a force is a superstition, like fate. It appears only in verse 6, among the things people hand their lives to. Unchosen circumstances are things that "simply happen".
- **Verse 1** opens bluntly. Is that right for the start of a chapter, or should it arrive a little later?
- **Verses 18–19** carry the no-gods difference: forgiveness lives with the people you hurt.
- **Reference test:** as chapter 1 of a book called *Responsibility*, verse 4 would be cited as *Responsibility 1:4*.
