# Trial chapter: Responsibility

Status: **TRIAL DRAFT, NOT APPROVED TEXT.** No book assigned.

Version 3 (11 Nov 2026), checked against `EDITORIAL/checklist.md`. Version history is kept in git:
- v1 was too negative;
- v2 was preachy, slogan-like, and overclaimed in verse 10.

---

1. There are no gods.
2. We have this world, this life and each other.
3. What we make of them is up to us.
4. People have long found comfort in the thought that everything was decided before they were born.
5. They have handed their lives to gods and to fate, to luck and to the stars, to "meant to be", to whoever was giving the orders.
6. Some hand their lives to their own nature, and say, "That's just how I am."
7. It is an understandable thing to do, because choosing is hard.
8. But the choices were always ours.
9. Every kindness we have ever received came from someone who chose to give it.
10. The neighbour who brought food when someone died. The stranger who stopped. The friend who said sorry first.
11. Every cruelty was chosen too.
12. Courage and excuses, kindness and harm, belong to the people who choose them.
13. Some things simply happen.
14. Where we are born, to whom, and in what body, all come before any choice of ours.
15. Everyone begins where they find themselves.
16. What we do from there is ours.
17. A single choice can change a day, a friendship, sometimes a life.
18. When we do harm, forgiveness lives with the people we have hurt.
19. It is theirs to give, and ours to ask for.
20. When we do good, the reward is already there, in the people it reaches.
21. This is a great deal to carry.
22. It is lighter carried together.
23. We answer for ourselves, and we answer to each other.
24. So we look after each other.
25. Some would call this a burden.
26. It is what makes a life our own.
