# Needs map: the human moments the Bible serves

Status: **Research note / working tool.** Created 11 Nov 2026.

**Purpose:** The Bible has lasted partly because it has something for every big moment of a life. This map lists widely used passages, the human need each one serves, and where The Teryn might meet the same need **with its own original writing**. We are not rewriting Bible verses (see the discussion of 11 Nov 2026).

**Notes on method**

- The passages are chosen from general knowledge of what is commonly read at ceremonies, quoted in popular culture and printed on cards and posters. This is **not a measured ranking**; verify before making any claims about popularity.
- Quotations are from the King James Version (1611), with a modern translation where its wording is the famous one.
- "Teryn book" refers to the brainstorm names in `IDEAS.md`. These are proposals only.

## What the map shows

### 1. The hardest moments (marked ⚠)

In these moments the Bible offers a **promise** The Teryn cannot honestly make: an afterlife, a divine plan, protection, "everything happens for a reason". These are where the Bible's grip is strongest, and they are where The Teryn will be judged.

The Teryn's answers must be honest, comforting, and must not argue. Possible directions:

- **"I am with thee"** → *you are not alone; you have each other.* This is your founding line ("this life and each other") doing real work.
- **Life after death** → what genuinely remains: love, memory, influence, the atoms of a body returning to the world.
- **A plan for your life** → no one has written your story; you get to.
- **Suffering has a purpose** → it doesn't need one, and you don't need to earn comfort.

These four answers are probably the most important passages The Teryn will ever contain.

### 2. Many famous passages are already universal

Ecclesiastes 3, "two are better than one", the golden rule, "love is patient": people of any belief can read these. The needs are human, not religious. That is encouraging: it means The Teryn can meet them fully.

### 3. Gaps in the current book list

The brainstorm in `IDEAS.md` has nothing for:

- **Blessings**: words for wishing someone well at a birth, a farewell, a new start or a meal.
- **Stories**: memorable narratives, especially ones children could be told. The current list is almost all reflection.
- **Words said together**: a short piece a group can say aloud from memory. This needs care, as it could feel too much like prayer.
- **Rest**: a day, or a habit, of stopping.

### 4. Courage needs a home

Fear and courage come up often enough that "Courage" may deserve its own book rather than merging into Kindness.
