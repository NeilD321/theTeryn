# The Teryn

**A book about life, not gods.**

A secular collection of books, chapters and numbered verses about existence, knowledge, human experience and how we live together.

> We have this universe, this planet, this life and each other. What we make of them is our responsibility.

(Opening line still being chosen. The book's final line is "There are no gods." See `VISION.md`.)

## Project status

**Concept and architecture stage.** No text of The Teryn has been written or approved.

## Conventions

- Every document states whether its content is **proposal**, **agreed** or **complete**.
- Agreed decisions change only through a new entry in `DECISIONS.md`.
- No material is attributed to real people without their involvement, and no quotation is used unverified.
