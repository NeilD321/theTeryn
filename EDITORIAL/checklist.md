# Draft checklist

Status: **Working tool** (created 11 Nov 2026 at the founder's request).

Every draft is checked against this list **before** it is shown to the founder, and the results are reported with the draft. Sources are the master brief (§ numbers refer to its sections) and the founder's directions in `DECISIONS.md`.

If a rule here conflicts with a later decision, the decision wins and this list is updated.

---

## A. What The Teryn is

1. **What we have, not what we lack** (§17). Count the negatives ("no", "not", "never", "no one", "nothing", "without"). "There are no gods" is the one negative the work needs; any other must earn its place.
2. **A celebration of existence** (§17). Does the passage leave the reader with more, not less?
3. **State it, don't argue it** (decision 11 Nov). Plainly stating there are no gods is fine. Arguing, mocking or belittling believers is not. Test: would a thoughtful religious reader feel *disagreed with* (fine) or *looked down on* (not fine)?
4. **Not self-help cliché** (§13).

## B. Style (§8)

5. **No archaic or biblical English**: no thee, unto, lo, yea, or inverted word order. No verses starting "And", "Nor" or "For" just for rhythm.
6. **No preaching.** Prefer "we" to telling "you" what to do. Describe rather than command. An instruction must earn its place.
7. **No empty slogans.** Every line says something specific and true. Test: on a fridge magnet with no context, would it mean almost nothing? Prefer concrete images to abstractions.
8. **Not every verse a proverb.** Connecting verses that carry the reader forward are good.
9. **No unsupported certainty.** Check every "every", "all", "always", "never" and "no one". Is it actually true?
10. **No excessive solemnity, pretension or jargon.**
11. **Science is not moral commandment.**

## C. Principles (§9)

12. **Reality matters.** Claims about the world are consistent with evidence; uncertainty is admitted.
13. **No overstating metaphysics.** E.g. say "our choices are ours", not "science proves free will".

## D. Audience (§12)

14. **An intelligent teenager can follow it**, and an educated adult still finds it rewarding.
15. **No unnecessary Western assumptions** in examples, images or idioms.

## E. Founder's directions (`DECISIONS.md`)

16. **Short verses, one per line**, no long paragraphs, and the chapter reads straight through as a connected whole.
17. **Not funny** (11 Nov).
18. **Central theme: responsibility**, including the no-gods difference (no plan, no final judge, forgiveness only from people) where relevant.
19. **No luck as a force.** Unchosen circumstances "simply happen".
20. **Kind.** No blaming people for what they did not choose.

## F. Research (§15)

21. **Quotations verified**; nothing invented; nothing written in a real person's voice.

## G. Final tests

22. **Read-aloud test.** Would it work spoken at a funeral, a naming or a gathering?
23. **Founder's fragments.** If a line the founder liked has been changed, say so.
